## 🌟 Executive Overview

In the streaming intelligence landscape, leadership teams require fast, clutter-free visual analytics to guide content acquisition, market expansion, and portfolio balancing. 

This dashboard addresses that need by combining **10+ dynamic Chart.js visualizations**, a **real-time KPI summary bar**, a **sticky multi-parameter filter panel**, and an **automated executive intelligence engine** that recalculates key metrics on the fly.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 📊 **Dynamic KPI Bar**: Instantly tracks Total Titles, Movie vs. TV Split, Director Coverage, Global Reach (Countries), and Average Release Year.
* 🎛️ **Sticky Global Filter Panel**: Real-time multi-dimensional slicing by Content Type, Release Year, Country, Rating, Genre, and Title Search with single-click reset.
* 📈 **10 Visual Analytics Charts**:
  * **Catalog Growth Trajectory Over Time**: Line chart tracking ingestion history.
  * **Format Ratio Split**: Doughnut chart analyzing film vs. episodic balance.
  * **Top Producing Countries**: Geographic distribution of catalog origin.
  * **Maturity Rating Demographics**: Volume split across age classifications.
  * **Top 10 Genre Categories**: Horizontal bar breakdown of popular genres.
  * **Production Release Year Trend**: Content freshness and archival depth.
  * **Movie Duration Buckets**: Runtime histogram for feature films.
  * **Regional Format Strategy Matrix**: Stacked bar comparison across top hubs.
  * **Prolific Directors Frequency**: Top creators in current active selection.
  * **Ingestion Seasonality**: Monthly distribution of platform additions.
* 💡 **Automated Executive Insights Engine**: Context-aware sidebar that dynamically generates strategic callouts regarding Content Dominance, Portfolio Risk, and Recency Velocity.
* 🏆 **Most Watched Content Index**: Sortable, searchable performance ranking table utilizing algorithmic popularity proxy scoring.
* 📁 **Universal Dataset Parser**: Pre-loaded with catalog records and equipped with drag-and-drop CSV upload capabilities powered by PapaParse.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Frontend Framework**: Vanilla HTML5, Modern CSS3 (CSS Grid & Flexbox)
* **Data Visualization**: [Chart.js (v4.x)](https://www.chartjs.org/) via CDN
* **Data Parsing**: [PapaParse](https://www.papaparse.com/) for zero-dependency client-side CSV processing
* **Design System**: Corporate Executive Theme (Navy `#1a2744`, Slate `#e2e8f0`, Performance Accents in Teal `#0d9488`, Amber `#d97706`, and Red `#dc2626`)
